# Privacy policy

The public control/skill layer stores methods only. Real personal facts stay in the private data layer and never flow back automatically.

Before persistence:

1. Separate source facts, reviewed notes, outputs, inference and private material.
2. Scan supported text files for identity, contact, address, credential, token, private-key, payment and account patterns.
3. Redact previews in reports.
4. Mark unsupported binary files for manual review.
5. Exclude raw medical/identity records and credentials from ordinary outputs by default.

A clean text scan is evidence, not proof that a dataset is safe.
